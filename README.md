## EduAdvisor — AI-Powered Academic Advising Assistant
> Answers student questions about courses, prerequisites, and graduation
> requirements, grounded in official university PDF documents.
## Problem Statement
Students struggle to get timely, accurate academic advice. Advisors are overloaded.
EduAdvisor provides instant, document-grounded answers 24/7.
## Evidence of the Problem
- Course, policy, and graduation information is distributed across multiple official documents.
- A single advising question may require students to consult more than one source.
## Initial Related Work / Existing Approaches
- Human academic advising — contextual, but availability depends on staff time.
- Static FAQ/policy pages — authoritative, but fragmented and difficult to navigate.
- General-purpose AI assistants — flexible, but may not be grounded in official university documents.
## Engineering Gap / Limitation
Existing approaches are fragmented, depend on staff availability, or may not ground answers consistently in approved university documents.
## Project Objectives
- O1: Design a document-grounded AI academic advising solution.
- O2: Develop the solution with retrieval and appropriate guardrails.
- O3: Evaluate answer quality, groundedness, latency, and safety.
## Target Users
Undergraduate students planning course registration and graduation.
## Data Sources
- Course Catalogue PDF (university website)
- Student Handbook PDF (university website)
- Graduation Requirements PDF (faculty office)
